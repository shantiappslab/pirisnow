Frequently asked questions

When does the ski season start in the Pyrenees?

The 2026-27 season starts on 25 November with Luz Ardiden. Most resorts open between late November and early December, depending on snow, and close in April (the last one, Cauterets (Cirque du Lys + Pont d'Espagne), on 18 April).

Where is there snow this weekend?

Every day we rank the resorts by the snow, weather, wind and crowds expected on Saturday and Sunday. See "Weekend" or the app, which works it out from your city.

Is PiriSnow free?

Yes. The report for all 73 resorts, the map, the forecast and the alerts are free, with no sign-up and no ads. Optional plans add more alerts and offline maps.

Where does the data come from?

From each resort's official report, DWD and ECMWF forecasts worked out for each resort's altitude, the official avalanche bulletins and road conditions from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Updated every morning.

Is there an iPhone version?

For now PiriSnow is on Android. On iPhone you can use this website, which has the report for every resort.
