Frequently asked questions

When does the ski season start in the Pyrenees?

The 2026-27 season starts on 25 November with Luz Ardiden. Most resorts open between late November and early December, depending on snow, and close in April (the last one, Cauterets (Cirque du Lys + Pont d'Espagne), on 18 April).

Where is the best snow today?

Every day we rank the resorts by weather, snow depth, fresh snow and quality, for today, tomorrow and the weekend. See "Where to go?"; in the app, it works it out from your city or location.

Is PiriSnow free?

Yes, it's a free download on Google Play with no sign-up. It has the report for all 70 resorts, the map, the forecast and the alerts.

Where does the data come from?

From each resort's official report, ECMWF, AIFS, ICON (DWD) and ARPEGE forecasts worked out for each resort's altitude, the official avalanche bulletins and road conditions from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Updated twice a day, in the morning and at midday.

How many ski resorts are there in the Pyrenees?

PiriSnow covers 70 ski resorts in the Pyrenees: 30 in Spain (Catalonia, Aragon and Navarre), 4 in Andorra and 36 in France, for alpine and cross-country skiing. This website has the snow report for all of them.

Is there an iPhone version?

For now PiriSnow is on Android. On iPhone you can use this website, which has the report for every resort.
