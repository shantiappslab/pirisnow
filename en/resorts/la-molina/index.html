How to get to La Molina

By road, right now

  • Toses Pass · N-260: No incidents
  • Cadí Tunnel · C-16: No incidents
  • Access to La Molina and Masella (Alp) · GIV-4033: No incidents

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Roads to the slopes today ›

Without a car

  • Train: Rodalies R3 to La Molina station · from Barcelona · 180 min
    Skitren combined ticket (train + bus to the slopes + lift pass; €53 in 2025-26). Track-doubling works on the R3: check for disruptions. ›
  • Shuttle: Shuttle bus from the train station to the slopes · from La Molina train station
    €5; free with Skitren or a season pass. ›
  • Bus: Sagalés ski bus + lift pass · from Barcelona and Vallès · 150 min
    Return bus with guide, lift pass and insurance (€66–80 in 2025-26). Information: Sagalés 935 931 112. ›
  • Bus: Bus Blanc Puigcerdà – La Molina – Masella · from Puigcerdà
    Seasonal Cerdanya bus to the slopes; timetables as a PDF on the website. ›
