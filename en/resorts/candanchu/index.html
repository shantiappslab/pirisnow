By road, right now

  • Somport Tunnel · N-330: Caution (chains or winter tyres)

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Roads to the slopes today ›

Without a car

  • Train: Renfe train Zaragoza – Huesca – Jaca – Canfranc · from Zaragoza
    Few trains a day (about 2). From Canfranc-Estación to Candanchú, bus T01 (about 10 min). ›
  • Bus: Coach T01 Jaca – Canfranc – Candanchú – Astún · from Jaca · 50 min
    About 5 services a day; stops at Candanchú Rioseta and Candanchú Marquesina. ›
  • Shuttle: Free 100K bus Candanchú – Astún · from Astún
    Free link between the two resorts of the 100K ski area. ›
