How to get to Formigal-Panticosa

By road, right now

  • Portalet Pass · A-136: No incidents
  • A-136 Biescas–Formigal: No incidents

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Roads to the slopes today ›

Without a car

  • Bus: Coach T01 Zaragoza – Huesca – Sabiñánigo – Formigal · from Zaragoza · 210 min
    Aragon public network (line T01). Stops at Huesca, Sabiñánigo, Biescas, Panticosa and Sallent. ›
  • Bus: Coach T01 Jaca – Sabiñánigo – Formigal · from Jaca / Sabiñánigo · 90 min
    Sabiñánigo and Jaca have Renfe trains from Zaragoza (Canfranc line). ›
  • Shuttle: Free ski bus Formigal – Sallent – Panticosa · from Sallent de Gállego / Panticosa
    Free Aramón service: from Panticosa residential areas and hotels to the gondola, and from Sallent/Formigal to the slopes. ›
