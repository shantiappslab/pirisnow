How to get to Vall de Núria

By road, right now

  • N-260 Ripoll–Ribes (access to Núria): Caution (roadworks)

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Roads to the slopes today ›

Without a car

  • Train: Rodalies R3 to Ribes de Freser (Ribes Enllaç) · from Barcelona · 140 min
    Ski Tren and Trenatura combined tickets (R3 + rack railway). Works on the R3: check for disruptions. ›
  • Rack railway: Núria rack railway · from Ribes Enllaç / Ribes Vila / Queralbs · 40 min
    The only access to the valley, which is car-free. Free parking at the three access stations. ›
