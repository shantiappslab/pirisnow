How to get to Astún

By road, right now

  • Somport Pass · N-330: No incidents
  • Somport Tunnel · N-330: No incidents

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Roads to the slopes today ›

Without a car

  • Train: Renfe train Zaragoza – Huesca – Jaca – Canfranc · from Zaragoza
    Few trains a day (about 2). At Jaca or Canfranc-Estación, bus T01 to Astún. ›
  • Bus: Coach T01 Jaca – Canfranc – Candanchú – Astún · from Jaca · 55 min
    About 5 services a day. Operated by Avanza since April 2026 (previously by the Mancomunidad Alto Valle del Aragón). ›
  • Shuttle: Free 100K bus Astún – Candanchú · from Candanchú
    Free link between the two resorts of the 100K ski area. ›
