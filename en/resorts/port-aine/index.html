How to get to Port Ainé

By road, right now

  • C-13 Pallars Sobirà: Caution (roadworks)
    Closed 23–5 h

Official incidents from the DGT, the Servei Català de Trànsit and Bison Futé. Roads to the slopes today ›

Without a car

  • Bus: ALSA ski bus + lift pass to Port Ainé · from Lleida, Balaguer, Tremp, La Pobla de Segur, Sort, Rialp
    2025-26 prices including lift pass: €45 (Lleida, Balaguer) and €40 (Tremp, La Pobla, Sort, Rialp). Buy from ALSA or on the bus. ›
  • Shuttle: Snow taxi Sort – Port Ainé · from Sort
    €2 per journey. Book the day before by 7 pm (+34 658 806 185). ›
